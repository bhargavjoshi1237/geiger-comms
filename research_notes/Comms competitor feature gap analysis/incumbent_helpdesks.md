# Incumbent Helpdesk Platforms Competing with Intercom — Feature Depth, AI, Pricing (state as of 21 Oct 2026)

> Scope: Zendesk, Freshworks (Freshdesk/Omni/Freshchat), HubSpot Service Hub, Salesforce (brief), Zoho Desk, Help Scout, Front, Gorgias, Kustomer, Gladly. AI-native startups and Intercom itself are covered by other researchers; Intercom appears here only as a comparison point, using the internal July 2026 census (`C:/Pro/geiger-comms/docs/competitor-feature-inventory.md`, scraped 2026-07-28) unless otherwise cited.
>
> Source-quality legend used below: **[primary]** = vendor page, release notes, or press release; **[3rd-party]** = review/implementation blog, often written by a competitor (eesel, Macha, Gorgias, Gladly, IrisAgent and Richpanel all sell competing products or services); **[internal]** = earlier Geiger research. Dates in brackets mark when a fact was true if it predates June 2026.

## Key Question 1 — Per vendor: feature variety and depth across the support cycle (channels, workspace, self-service, automation, CRM, proactive, reporting, QA/WFM, voice, integrations, admin) and packaging

### Takeaway
Zendesk remains the widest and deepest incumbent. It is the only one in this set that bundles native WFM (basic WFM free on Suite Professional+ since July 2026), a full QA product, a native contact center, approval workflows, sandbox and custom objects. Freshdesk and Zoho Desk match much of that breadth for less money. HubSpot wins on CRM-native customer success. Help Scout, Front and Gorgias are deliberately narrower but deeper in one dimension each: simplicity, collaboration, and ecommerce. Kustomer and Gladly compete on a customer-centric data model rather than breadth.

### Inferences
- Zendesk is turning its paid add-ons (WFM, QA-lite "Quality Score", merge suggestions) into tier features on Suite Professional. That raises the baseline a challenger must meet at the ~$115/agent price point.
- Most of the incumbent release volume from June to October 2026 is AI-agent plumbing (procedures, action flows, connectors, versioning) and data-model depth (multi-lookup fields, custom-object sync). Classic inbox features are mostly polish.
- Help Scout spent 2026 closing table-stakes gaps (WhatsApp, SMS, SLAs, round robin, availability, customer portal). That suggests these are now the minimum even for "simple" SMB helpdesks.

## Key Question 2 — Per vendor: 3–6 genuinely distinctive features, and where each is deeper or shallower than Intercom

### Takeaway
Each incumbent beats Intercom on one axis. Zendesk wins on enterprise operations (WFM, QA calibration, approval workflows, sandbox, contact center, employee service). HubSpot wins on CRM-native success (health scores, NRR/GRR, product usage). Front wins on collaboration and external-agent orchestration, Gorgias on ecommerce order actions and per-ticket pricing, Gladly and Kustomer on person-centric data, and Freshdesk and Zoho on value and process builders. Intercom stays deeper than all of them on proactive in-product messaging (tours, checklists, posts, series) and on an AI agent sold standalone into rival helpdesks.
