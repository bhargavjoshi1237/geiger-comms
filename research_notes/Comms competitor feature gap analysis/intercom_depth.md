## Q1. Fin AI Agent — version, channels, Procedures/Guidance, actions, multilingual, testing, analytics, standalone, pricing, certifications, and what is new since mid-2025

### Takeaway
The current release is **Fin 4** (Oct 2026), running on in-house **Apex 2.0** models. It is one "Customer Agent" with four modes (**Service, Sales, Ecommerce, Success**) that runs on chat, email, voice, WhatsApp, SMS, Instagram, Facebook, Discord, Slack, Telegram and API. Its control surface is now a full sub-application with these layers:
- **Train:** Guidance, Content, Procedures, Skills, Attributes, Audiences, Identities
- **Test:** Simulations/Evals, Releases, A/B, rollout
- **Deploy**
- **Analyze:** Performance, CX Score, Topics, Trends, Recommendations, Incident Detection, Monitors/Scorecards
- **Operator:** an AI admin agent that edits all of the above via PR-style proposals

Price is $0.99 per *outcome* (since 12 Mar 2026), or $9.99 per qualification for Fin for Sales.

### Cited Findings
**Versions and models**
- **Fin 4** has three models. **Apex 2.0** (answer generation) gets +8.6% confirmed resolutions vs Apex 1.0. **Apex Flash 2.0** (voice) answers 24% faster than Flash 1.0. **Apex for Procedures** gets +15% confirmed resolutions vs "Sonnet 5". — [fin.ai/fin4](https://fin.ai/fin4); [fin.ai](https://fin.ai/)
- Fin Apex 1.0, the first custom model, shipped March 2026 and "generates the final answer for every customer". — [fin.ai/updates](https://fin.ai/updates)
- Model providers: OpenAI, Anthropic, Google and Intercom's own models, with automatic switching between AI providers for resilience. — [fin.ai/trust-reliability](https://fin.ai/trust-reliability)

**Modes / roles (one Customer Agent)**

**Human in the Loop (HITL)**
- HITL is in **Beta** on chat and email, Intercom helpdesk only:
  - Fin scores the confidence of each draft (badge Low → Very High). Below a threshold on matching rules, it holds the answer and asks a teammate under "Fin for Service › Needs teammate input".
  - The teammate can approve the draft, give Fin an answer (Fin redrafts for approval), or take over with a reason.
  - Config: conditions/topic, channels, threshold, who to ask (teammate/team using its assignment method), and a time limit (default 24h, then send the draft).
  - Reporting: a HITL report with time-to-action per teammate.
  - Billing: it is billed as a normal outcome only if Fin sends the resolution.
  — [Intercom help: Human in the Loop](https://www.intercom.com/help/en/articles/16198358-ask-a-teammate-for-help-with-email-replies-human-in-the-loop)
- Fin 4 "HITL 2nd gen": Fin writes and sends the final reply and owns the resolution. A teammate can take over at any time, and Fin stays involved until they do. — [fin.ai/fin4](https://fin.ai/fin4)

**Testing**
- Evals: scenarios built from real conversations. One LLM plays the customer and another judges handoff timing, content/data use and adherence.
- Regression: reruns grouped by topic, on demand or on a schedule.
- **Releases**: changes are drafted in a separate space and need approval.
- Rollout: live-traffic validation, gradual rollout with instant rollback, and **A/B tests** of Fin versions on resolution rate or CSAT.
- Monitors can turn failing conversations into new Evals.
— [fin.ai/testing](https://fin.ai/testing)
- Older testing features: Fin Preview (2023, improved Oct 2024), Batch Test (Mar 2025), Fin over Email preview (Apr 2026). — [fin.ai/updates](https://fin.ai/updates)

## Q12. Pricing and packaging (October 2026)

### Takeaway
Seats are **Essential $29/$39**, **Advanced $85/$99** and **Expert $132/$139** (annual/monthly) per seat. Fin costs **$0.99/outcome** ($9.99 per sales qualification). Add-ons:
- **Copilot**: $29/$35 per agent.
- **Pro**: $0.20/conversation, 500 minimum = $100/mo; includes CX Score, Topics, Trends, Recommendations, Incident Detection, Monitors, Scorecards, Operator credits and Workforce Management.
- **Proactive Support Plus**: $99/mo, 500 messages, tiered overage.

Usage channels are pay-as-you-go. Standalone Fin needs no seats, with a minimum of 50 outcomes per month.

## Q13. Known weaknesses (user reviews and documented limits)

### Takeaway
The dominant complaint is **pricing**: confusing, frequent increases, costly add-ons, and seat plus per-outcome stacking that grows with AI success. Next come **bugs/outages and slow support**, then setup complexity and one-way integrations. The help docs themselves expose many hard limits a clone can beat.

### Cited Findings
- Capterra sentiment: 64% negative among 211 reviews mentioning pricing (frequent increases, add-on costs). 71% negative among 91 mentioning bugs/outages. Overall rating 4.5 (1,133 reviews). Many reviews date from 2018–2023. — [Capterra: Intercom reviews](https://www.capterra.com/p/134347/Intercom/reviews/) *(search summary)*
- G2 is about 4.5/5 on roughly 3,855–3,893 reviews. Trustpilot is about 1.9/5 on 950+ reviews, citing slow support and billing changes after migration to new pricing. HubSpot integration is one-way. Setup is complex and must be updated with product changes. — [YourGPT Intercom review](https://yourgpt.ai/blog/comparison/intercom-review) *(3rd-party/vendor-affiliated, search summary)*; [Kayako](https://kayako.com/?p=17293) *(3rd-party competitor, search summary)*
- Seat fees plus $0.99 per resolution mean bills grow as the AI performs better. The same source reports vendor-claimed 76% deflection vs 38–50% in independent tests (unverified). — [Unthread: Intercom Review 2026](https://unthread.io/blog/intercom-review/) *(3rd-party competitor blog, search summary)*
- Gartner Peer Insights reviews from Mar–Apr 2026 criticize email features (scheduling visibility, formatting) and call the UI "primitive". — [Gartner Peer Insights](https://www.gartner.com/reviews/market/social-customer-service-applications/vendor/intercom/product/intercom-customer-service-platform) *(search summary)*
- A community user reports office-hours math turned a 10-day SLA target into about 30 calendar days. — [Community: How to set SLAs properly](https://community.intercom.com/support-operations-56/how-to-set-sla-s-properly-13426?tid=13426&fid=56) *(search summary)*
- Documented hard limits from Intercom's own docs:
  - **SLAs**: one SLA per conversation; can't remove a target or edit archived SLAs. — [Set SLAs](https://www.intercom.com/help/en/articles/6546152-set-slas-for-conversations-and-tickets)

  - **Macros**: can't reply and note at once, no pause, no actions on mobile. — [Inbox FAQs](https://www.intercom.com/help/en/articles/8838656-inbox-faqs) *(search summary)*

  - **Data**: companies can't be CSV-imported; custom objects are immutable and can't be nested. — [Import user data](https://www.intercom.com/help/en/articles/177-import-your-user-data-into-intercom) *(search summary)*; [Custom Objects FAQs](https://www.intercom.com/help/en/articles/6298293-custom-actions-and-objects-faqs) *(search summary)*

  - **Lite seats**: can't search. — [Seats](https://www.intercom.com/help/en/articles/8205716-seats) *(search summary)*

### Inferences
- Strongest clone positioning: transparent and simple pricing (no add-on stack, no AI tax that scales with success), enterprise features (SLA, balanced routing, roles, SSO) not locked to the top tier, editable data schemas, and customizable shortcuts.
- The Salesforce acquisition (closed Sep 2026) is a new source of buyer uncertainty: roadmap, pricing and Salesforce-centricity. This is inference; I found no reviews about it yet.

### Gaps
- I could not fetch G2 directly for current, 2026-only con themes. Reporting-specific complaints in 2026 reviews were not found. The only reporting criticism located is from 2020, so "reporting is weak" is **not** supported by current evidence.
